Smart And Irrigation System

Ye ek fully automatic irrigation system hai jo aapke garden ya khet ko smart banata hai. Ise aap apne mobile app se kahin bhi baithkar control kar sakte hain. Ismein real-time on/off aur custom timer scheduling jaise features hain, jo pani ki bachat aur paudhon ki sahi dekhbhal ensure karte hain. Manual mehnat chhodein aur is advanced automation ke saath apne irrigation ko effortless banayein!

Smart And Fogger System

Ye fully automatic fogger system advanced technology ke saath aata hai, jise aap duniya mein kahin se bhi apne mobile app ke zariye control kar sakte hain. Ismein on/off ke saath-saath smart timer scheduling ka feature hai, jo aapke kaam ko asaan banata hai. Ghar ho ya farm, iska auto-mode precision watering aur cooling ensure karta hai. Smart banen, aur manually switch on-off karne ki tension se mukti payen!

Smart And Music Fountain

A Smart Music Fountain technology aur art ka ek badhiya mix hai, jo music ke beats aur frequencies ke saath water pumps aur RGB LEDs ko sync karta hai. Microcontroller ka use karke, ye ek mesmerizing "dancing water" effect create karta hai jahan spray ki height aur colors gaane ke rhythm ke hisaab se badalte hain. Ye smart decor ke liye ek futuristic aur high-tech project hai.
